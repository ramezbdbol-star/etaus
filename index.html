Questions

Before you get in touch

I have never bought from China. Can you still help?

Yes. We work with buyers who have prior experience and with buyers who have none, and we give you on-site assistance in China.

Which languages do you work in?

Our team is bilingual and we offer services in Spanish, English, French and Chinese.

I already have a supplier. Can you check the goods?

Yes. If you have found a supplier or confirmed an order, we can go and verify the supplier or inspect the goods before any further action. Inspecting goods is an essential step before exporting them from China.

Can someone come with me to factories, markets or trade shows?

Yes. You can hire a personal Chinese interpreter. We accompany you to factories, wholesale markets and trade shows in China.

Where are you based?
